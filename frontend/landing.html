How It Works

From EEG File to Risk Score in 3 Steps

A simple, transparent process powered by state-of-the-art deep learning.

1
Upload Your EDF File
Upload a standard EDF/EDF+ format EEG recording from your clinical device. The system supports 19-channel montages at any sample rate.
2
AI Analyzes the Data
Our CNN-LSTM model resamples to 128 Hz, generates STFT spectrograms from 5-second windows, and runs inference across all epochs.
3
Review Your Results
Get a depression risk percentage, MDD classification, and epoch risk charts. Chat with our AI assistant to understand your report.
